# Scope of the open-source license

The repository's MIT license applies only to project-authored software and documentation unless a file states otherwise.

It does not license or transfer rights in:

- WRDS, OptionMetrics, Cboe, exchange, or other third-party data;
- vendor schemas, manuals, proprietary methodologies, names, or marks;
- credentials or access mechanisms;
- empirical extracts or outputs whose dissemination is contractually restricted; or
- third-party publications linked or cited by the project.

The deterministic public demo is project-authored synthetic data. Licensed research data and contract-sensitive outputs are outside the open-source license.
